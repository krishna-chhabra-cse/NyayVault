Architecture & Defenses

Attacking The Risk

Risk 1: Evidence Tampering

If a file is changed, the chain of custody is broken and the case is ruined.

Our Defense

Background workers instantly lock original files in AWS S3 and immutably log the SHA-256 hash in PostgreSQL.

Risk 2: AI Hallucination

If the AI invents a legal fact or misquotes evidence, it's illegal.

Our Defense

Strict Evidence-Grounded RAG. The AI is hard-coded to ONLY generate answers derived from the encrypted vector database.
